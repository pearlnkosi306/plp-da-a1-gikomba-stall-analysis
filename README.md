# Gikomba Market Stall Performance Analysis

**Author:** [Buhle Pearl Nkosi] · PLP Data Analytics [1MillionDevs Cohort]

## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded her daily sales in a notebook for the last two weeks, along with a rough record of her stock. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, what she should restock, and which days she should focus on. This project uses core Python only (no pandas or NumPy) to turn her 14 days of sales and her stock register into clear answers and recommendations.

## Top Three Findings

1. **The business is growing.** Sales rose from KES 22,050 in week 1 to KES 24,400 in week 2, a **10.7%** increase (total KES 46,450 over 14 days).
2. **Weekends are far stronger.** Weekend days average KES 4,900 against KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is **82.5% higher**.
3. **Two products need restocking now.** Tomatoes (18 kg vs a 25 kg reorder level) and Sukuma (9 kg vs 15 kg) are below their reorder levels, while Avocado has the best gross margin at **42.9%**.
